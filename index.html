How it works

The converter keeps your physical cm/360° the same.

Sensitivity ↓ Source yaw ↓ cm/360° ↓ Target yaw ↓ Target sensitivity
⚠️ This converter matches horizontal hipfire sensitivity. ADS, scopes, FOV and game-specific multipliers can feel different.

What is cm/360°?

cm/360° is the physical distance your mouse must travel to make a complete 360° turn in-game.

Matching cm/360° between games is a common way to keep the same physical mouse movement.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is cm/360°?

CM/360° is the physical distance your mouse must travel to make a complete 360° turn in-game.

Do I need to keep the same DPI?

No. The converter compensates when the target DPI is different. Keeping the same DPI simply makes the setup easier to understand.

Will my ADS sensitivity be exactly the same?

Not necessarily. This tool focuses on horizontal hipfire sensitivity. ADS, scopes and FOV settings can behave differently between games.

Why are some conversions less certain?

Some games have additional aiming systems, scope multipliers or different sensitivity scales. These are marked with a lower confidence level.

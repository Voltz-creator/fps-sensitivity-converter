FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is cm/360°?

CM/360° is the physical distance your mouse must travel to make a complete 360° turn in-game.

Do I need to keep the same DPI?

No. The converter can compensate when your target DPI is different. Keeping the same DPI simply makes the setup easier to understand.

Will my ADS sensitivity be exactly the same?

Not necessarily. This tool currently focuses on horizontal hipfire sensitivity. ADS, scopes and FOV settings can behave differently between games.

Why are some conversions less certain?

Some games use additional sensitivity systems, ADS multipliers, scopes or different sensitivity scales. Those games are therefore marked with a lower confidence level.

ABOUT

About FPS Sensitivity Converter

FPS Sensitivity Converter is a free tool designed to help players transfer their mouse sensitivity between different FPS games.

The main goal is to keep a similar physical mouse movement by matching CM/360° between games.

The calculator is designed to be simple, fast and usable on both desktop and mobile devices.

Results may vary depending on game-specific settings, FOV, ADS multipliers and sensitivity systems.

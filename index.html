What is CM/360°?

CM/360° is the physical distance your mouse must travel to make a complete 360° turn in-game.

Matching CM/360° helps reproduce the same physical mouse movement between different FPS games.

The visual feeling can still differ because games use different FOV systems, ADS behavior and sensitivity settings.

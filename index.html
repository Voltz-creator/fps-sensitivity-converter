❓ FAQ
What is CM/360°?

CM/360° is the physical distance your mouse must travel to make a complete 360° rotation in a game. Matching CM/360° is a common way to keep a similar physical mouse sensitivity between FPS games.

Does DPI affect the conversion?

DPI affects the relationship between your physical mouse movement and your in-game sensitivity. This converter allows different source and target DPI values and compensates for the difference.

Will my aim feel exactly the same?

The converter targets horizontal hipfire CM/360°. ADS, scopes, FOV, zoom multipliers and game-specific settings can make the aiming experience feel different.

Which games are supported?

The converter currently supports Valorant, Counter-Strike 2, Apex Legends, Overwatch 2, Fortnite, Rainbow Six Siege, Call of Duty / Warzone, THE FINALS, Destiny 2, Deadlock and Marvel Rivals.

Why are some conversions marked medium confidence?

Some games have additional aiming systems, different sensitivity scales or game-specific settings. Those results should therefore be physically verified in-game.

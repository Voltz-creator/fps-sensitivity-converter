What is CM/360°?

CM/360° is the physical distance your mouse must travel to make a complete 360° turn in-game.

Matching CM/360° is a common way to reproduce similar physical mouse movement between FPS games.

However, the visual feeling can still differ because games use different FOV systems, ADS behavior and sensitivity settings.

Privacy Policy

How FPS Sensitivity Converter handles information.

1. About this website

FPS Sensitivity Converter is a free online tool designed to help players convert mouse sensitivity settings between supported FPS games.

The website does not require users to create an account or register in order to use the converter.

2. Information processed by the converter

The converter allows users to enter information such as game selections, mouse sensitivity and DPI values.

These values are used to perform the requested sensitivity calculations. The converter is designed to perform these calculations directly in the user's browser.

FPS Sensitivity Converter does not require users to provide their name, address, telephone number or other personal information in order to use the converter.

3. Personal data

The converter does not intentionally collect personal information from users through the sensitivity calculation itself.

If a user voluntarily contacts the website through the Contact page, the information provided in that message, such as an email address, may be used only to respond to the request.

Such information is not intentionally used for advertising or sold to third parties.

4. Cookies and tracking technologies

At the time of publication of this policy, FPS Sensitivity Converter does not intentionally use advertising cookies, analytics cookies or other non-essential tracking technologies on the website.

If non-essential cookies or tracking technologies are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated and the appropriate consent mechanism will be implemented where required by applicable law.

In particular, non-essential trackers that require consent will not be used before the required consent has been obtained.

5. External links

This website may contain links to external websites or services. These websites have their own privacy policies and practices.

FPS Sensitivity Converter is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

6. Data retention

Information voluntarily sent through the Contact page may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request and handle any related communication.

The website does not intentionally create user accounts or maintain a personal profile for users of the converter.

7. Your rights

Where applicable under data protection law, users may have rights concerning their personal data, including rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and data portability depending on the applicable legal basis and circumstances.

Users may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL).

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the processing of personal data, please use the Contact page.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the website, its functionality, its services or its data practices change.

The latest version of this policy will always be available on this page.
